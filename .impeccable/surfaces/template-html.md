# Surface brief: lieux de mariage (index.html)

Mode: Operate (tri de lieux, usage mobile le soir). Surface: single page, map + photo cards + fiche popup.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A night-motorway sign world. Every venue is a stop on the way: the signature move is the direction panel (blue sign, white inner border) that gives Lyon and Saint-Étienne drive times and km on each card and fiche. Refuses the cream-serif wedding page and the generic photo-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Night ground (blue-black), autoroute blue signs with white inner rule, brown tourist-sign badges for venue type, white signage type (Overpass, Highway-Gothic descended), amber for state (favori, active), tabular numerals. Light variant: pale road-map ground, same signs.

STORY: The couple opens it on the phone in the evening, sees big photos first, reads capacity / beds / price in a ruled strip, sees how far it is from Lyon and Saint-Étienne on a sign, stars favourites, writes notes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: blue header bar with title and count, horizontally scrolling zone chips, then full-width 3:2 venue photos with brown type badge, star toggle, below each a stats strip and the direction sign; bottom segmented Liste/Carte. Desktop: 520px list column beside a full-height dark map.

FORM: Highway signage (candidate 7 of my grounded list), seed key ae205500, borrowing the design annual's discipline of hairline rules and untinted photography.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
